Privacy Policy

Madame Celandra · Last updated October 5, 2026

Madame Celandra is a tarot reading app for entertainment purposes only. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and who it is shared with. We keep this to the minimum the app needs to work.

What we collect

  • Your Amazon user ID. Amazon gives the app an anonymous ID for your Amazon account. We use it to keep track of how many readings you have left, so your readings follow you if you reinstall the app. We do not receive your name, email address, or payment details.
  • Your reading count and purchase receipts. We store how many readings you have and the receipt IDs of reading packs you buy, so each purchase is credited exactly once.
  • Your questions. The question you type, and the cards you draw, are sent to our server to create your reading. We do not save your questions or readings on our server.
  • Your IP address. When a new account is created we briefly record a count of new accounts per IP address per day, to prevent abuse. We don't use it to identify you.

Who we share it with

  • Anthropic. Your question and drawn cards are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service, which writes Madame Celandra's reading. Anthropic processes this under its own commercial terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Please don't include personal details you wouldn't want shared in your questions.
  • Amazon. Purchases are handled by the Amazon Appstore. We send receipt IDs to Amazon to confirm purchases are genuine.
  • Cloudflare. Our server and database run on Cloudflare, which stores the reading counts and receipt IDs described above.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

On your device

The app may save a PDF of a reading to your device when you choose to download it. That file stays on your device.

Children

Madame Celandra is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

To have your reading count and purchase records deleted, email us from the address below and include the approximate date you first used the app. Deleting your record also removes any unused readings.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date above and post the new version on this page.
